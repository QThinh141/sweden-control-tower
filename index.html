NETCOMPANY PULSE

The brain

  • Sits on top of existing ERP / WMS / POS / carrier APIs
  • No master-data migration — no rip-and-replace
  • Battle-tested orchestration (Go-Ahead Norway, DSB)

Why it matters: Company A cannot rebuild its landscape in 8–12 weeks. PULSE layers on top.

ORTEC

The decision layer

  • AI ETA from delivery history, terrain, season & live traffic
  • Exception scoring — which orders need action now
  • +20–30% bookable delivery slots without cost increase

Evidence: proven at European retailers — more slots, fewer miles.

OPTRACK

The execution layer

  • Vehicle routing, real-time fleet tracking
  • Load optimisation — fewer trips, same promise
  • −6% trips (Brakes Group) · −15% fleet miles

Scope note: swap for carrier tracking APIs if Company A fully outsources the fleet.

💡 Design principle: we integrate what exists — we don't replace it. That is what makes an 8–12 week MVP realistic.
03 · THE MVP

Eight to twelve weeks. One flow. Three features.

Sweden only · Stockholm + Göteborg + Malmö · B2C home delivery — the flow where customer experience is most sensitive and re-delivery cost is highest.

✅ WHAT WE BUILD
1
Risk Dashboard

Every order whose ETA is slipping, on one screen — ranked, live, replacing 5 systems.

2
AI-recommended actions

Per order: 2–3 interventions with success probability and cost impact. One click to execute.

3
Customer portal

No app. An SMS link: live status, ETA and self-service re-booking in 10 seconds.

❌ WHAT WE DELIBERATELY DON'T DO
  • Denmark & Norway — later phases on the same core, new modules.
  • No ERP or POS rebuild — read-only integration via PULSE.
  • No full-chain AI — one flow done well beats five flows done badly.
  • No store operations changes — that is Norway's priority, not Sweden's.
